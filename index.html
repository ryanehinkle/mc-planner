MC Planner

Block-texture Minecraft planning

Left click draw/use tool · Right click erase · Middle click pan · Wheel zoom

Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Esc cancel shape preview · Space temporary pan

Each planner cell stores a real Minecraft block ID and renders its vanilla 16×16 texture. Materials are counted automatically.

Shape tools: click-drag for line/rectangle/circle/oval/triangle. Polygon: click points, double-click to finish. Curve: click-drag to set endpoints, then move/click to bend.

Mirror: choose the Mirror tool, set an angle, hover to preview the grid-aligned axis, then click once to place it. Add multiple axes for compound symmetry. Drawing tools reflect live across every placed axis.
